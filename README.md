# Union of Socialist Republics in Interland (USRI)

The **Union of Socialist Republics in Interland (USRI)** — commonly known as **Interland** — is a micronation founded on **7 August 2026**.

This repository contains the official USRI website, documentation, national symbols, language development, and cultural assets.

## Website

The repository now includes a responsive, static website with:

- 🇮🇳 Interland-inspired national branding and emblem
- 🏛️ USRI overview and the three republics
- 🎶 Built-in **Gloria a Interland** anthem player
- 📜 Spanish and English anthem lyrics
- 🏴 National symbols and motto
- 📱 Mobile-friendly navigation and layout
- 🌐 GitHub Pages deployment workflow

## 🏛️ Overview

The USRI is composed of three socialist republics:

- **Interland Socialist Republic** (Capital: *Kukil*)
- **Linterly Socialist Republic**
- **Cuikil Socialist Republic**

The nation is built on principles of unity, equality, and collective strength.

## 🎉 Independence Day

**7 August 2026**

Every year on this date, citizens of Interland gather in the capital **Kukil** to play and celebrate the national anthem *Gloria a Interland*.

## 🎶 National Anthem

**Title:** *Gloria a Interland*  
**Languages:** Spanish & English  
**Composer:** Union of Socialist Republics in Interland  
**Producer:** USRI

The anthem symbolizes unity between the republics and the strength of the Interlandian people.

The original anthem recording is included in this repository as `Gloria a Interland.mp3`.

## 🌐 Official Languages

The USRI recognizes two official languages:

- 🇪🇸 **Spanish** — Primary national language.
- 🇬🇧 **English** — Foreign communications language.

## 🏴 National Identity

The USRI stands for:

- **Unity** between republics
- **Strength** of the worker
- **Freedom** through solidarity
- **Cultural pride** expressed through language and music

**National Motto:**

> *Interland es eterna — Interland forever free.*
