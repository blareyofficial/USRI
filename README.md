# Union of Socialist Republics in Interland (USRI)

The **Union of Socialist Republics in Interland (USRI)** — commonly known as **Interland** — is a micronation founded on **7 August 2026**.  
This repository contains official documentation, language development, national symbols, and cultural assets of the USRI.

---

## 🏛️ Overview

The USRI is composed of three socialist republics:

- **Interland Socialist Republic** (Capital: *Kukil*)
- **Linterly Socialist Republic**
- **Cuikil Socialist Republic**

The nation is built on principles of unity, equality, and collective strength.

---

## 🎉 Independence Day

**7 August 2026**

Every year on this date, citizens of Interland gather in the capital **Kukil** to play and celebrate the national anthem *Gloria a Interland*.

---

## 🎶 National Anthem

**Title:** *Gloria a Interland*  
**Languages:** Spanish & English  
**Composer:** Union of Socialist Republics in Interland  
**Producer:** USRI

The anthem symbolizes unity between the republics and the strength of the Interlandian people.
