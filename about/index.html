THE UNION

About Interland

The Union of Socialist Republics in Interland — commonly known as Interland.

01

Founded

The USRI was founded on 7 August 2026.

02

The republics

Interland is composed of the Interland Socialist Republic, Linterly Socialist Republic and Cuikil Socialist Republic.

03

Capital

Kukil is the capital of the Interland Socialist Republic.

IDENTITY

Unity, strength and culture

The USRI describes its national identity through unity between republics, strength of the worker, freedom through solidarity and cultural pride expressed through language and music.

Spanish is the primary national language and English is used for foreign communications.

National motto
“Interland es eterna — Interland forever free.”
