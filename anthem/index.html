NATIONAL ANTHEM

Gloria a Interland

Spanish & English · Composer: Union of Socialist Republics in Interland · Producer: USRI

About the anthem

Gloria a Interland represents unity between the republics and the strength of the Interlandian people.

Español

En la tierra de Interland, donde nace la unión,
Se levanta nuestro pueblo con trabajo y con pasión.
Repúblicas hermanadas, marchando hacia el sol,
Con la fuerza del obrero y el poder del corazón.

English

From the mountains of Interland to the ocean’s shining line,
Every worker stands together, every spirit dares to rise.
Sister republics united, marching forward hand in hand,
Building hope with every sunrise, shaping one collective land.
