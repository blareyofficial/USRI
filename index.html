THE UNION

One union. Three republics.

The Union of Socialist Republics in Interland, commonly known as Interland, is a micronation composed of three socialist republics.

The national identity is built around unity between the republics, equality, collective strength, solidarity, cultural pride and the worker.

National motto
“Interland es eterna — Interland forever free.”
