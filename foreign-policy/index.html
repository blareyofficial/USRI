INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

Foreign Policy

Interland is a peaceful and diplomatic state. It maintains no armed forces of aggression and seeks relations founded on mutual recognition of sovereignty.

01

Declaration of Stance

Interland maintains a policy of Active Neutrality, engaging in diplomacy only where it serves the global peace and collective advancement. The Republic does not join military blocs, does not recognise claims advanced by force, and offers its good offices for mediation between other states.

02

Alliances

We seek partnerships with micronations that respect UI sovereignty and ideological purity. Alliances are entered by treaty, ratified in open sitting of the National Assembly, and published in full in the National Register — Interland concludes no secret agreements.

Tier I

Treaty of Recognition

Mutual acknowledgement of sovereignty and the exchange of standing correspondence.

Tier II

Compact of Cooperation

Shared cultural, archival and technical projects between assemblies.

Tier III

Pact of Common Peace

Binding mutual commitment to non-aggression and third-party mediation.

03

Neutrality

Interlandian neutrality is active, not passive. The Republic declines to take sides in the disputes of others but does not withdraw from the world: it observes, records, and offers its territory and archives as neutral ground for negotiation.

No Interlandian citizen may be conscripted for a foreign conflict, and no state asset may be lent to a belligerent party.

04

Cooperation

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs pursues cooperation in archival exchange, cartographic verification, currency and stamp issuance, cultural festivals, and the shared drafting of model legislation for small states. Requests for cooperation are answered within thirty days of receipt.

05

Micronational Diplomacy

Interland regards micronational diplomacy as a serious practice rather than an amusement. Its conduct rests on four standing rules:

Reciprocity

Recognition is offered to any state that offers it in return in good faith.

Documentation

Every accord is written, signed, sealed and published.

Non-interference

Interland comments on the internal affairs of no other state.

Continuity

Obligations survive changes of government; treaties bind the Union, not the ministry.

Diplomatic Correspondence

States wishing to open relations may address the Chancery of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at gov@interland.pntr.dev.
