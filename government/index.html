Government

Information about the institutions and organisation of the Union of Socialist Republics in Interland.

01

The Union

The USRI is a union composed of three socialist republics: the Interland Socialist Republic, Linterly Socialist Republic and Cuikil Socialist Republic.

02

Republics

The republics form the constituent parts of the union. The capital of the Interland Socialist Republic is Kukil.
